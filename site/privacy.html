Privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Pocket Passer is a free browser game. This page explains what it collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: you can play without an account; if you sign in, we keep your username and your scores, and nobody else sees your name or email.

If you play without signing in

  • Your settings and progress (sound, controls, how many free rounds you've played, your best scores) are stored in your own browser's local storage. They never leave your device.
  • We don't use cookies for tracking or advertising, and there are no ads.

If you sign in with Google

  • What Google shares with us: your Google account's email address, name and an account ID. We use them only to sign you in. Your Google name and email are never shown to other players.
  • Your username: you pick it. It's the only name shown on leaderboards.
  • Your game data: each throw's score, your finished rounds and your Today's 6 results, so you can see your stats and history and appear on the daily leaderboard.
  • Public: your username and your Today's 6 score on that day's leaderboard. Everything else is private to your account.

Analytics and error reports

  • We use Plausible Analytics, which is cookieless and doesn't collect personal data, to count visits and anonymous game events (for example, a round started or finished).
  • If the game hits an error, it sends a short technical report (the error message, what screen you were on, your browser type and screen size). It doesn't include your account or personal information.

Who handles the data

  • Supabase stores accounts and scores.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website.
  • Google handles sign-in.
  • Plausible provides analytics.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and everything linked to it at any time. Open a request on the project's GitHub issues page with your username (don't post your email publicly) and we'll delete it. You can also remove Pocket Passer's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account settings.

Children

Pocket Passer isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

About the game data

The plays in Pocket Passer are recreated from publicly released NFL Next Gen Stats tracking data. Pocket Passer isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the NFL or any team.
